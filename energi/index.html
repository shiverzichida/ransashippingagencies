BIOMASS RENEWABLE FUEL

PALM KERNEL SHELL (PKS)

Palm Kernel Shell is a premium by-product of palm oil production widely utilized in the renewable biomass energy industry. It is a fibrous, brownish-yellow material easily handled in bulk directly from mills to power plants. With a high calorific value (~4000 Kcal/kg) and low ash content, PKS is an ideal eco-friendly fuel alternative.

Common NamePalm Kernel Shell (PKS)
Nama LokalCangkang Sawit
SpesiesElaeis guineensis
Asal (Origin)Indonesia (Kalimantan Barat)
HS Code1404.90.91
Nilai Kalori~4,000 Kcal/kg
LIVESTOCK STOCKFEED

PALM KERNEL EXPELLER (PKE)

Palm Kernel Expeller (PKE) is a high-grade stockfeed by-product obtained during palm oil extraction. PKE contains high levels of crude protein and medium energy levels, making it a safe, nutrient-dense supplement for various livestock classes. Ideally suited for feeding alongside high-energy feeds like molasses or cereal meals.

Common NamePalm Kernel Expeller (PKE)
Nama LokalKernel Sawit / Bungkil Sawit
SpesiesElaeis guineensis
Asal (Origin)Indonesia (Kalimantan Barat)
HS Code2306.60.90
Kandungan ProteinHigh Crude Protein & Fiber
BOTANICAL COMMODITY

MITRAGYNA SPECIOSA (KRATOM)

Kratom (Mitragyna speciosa) is an endemic botanical plant originating from the tropical forests of West Kalimantan. Known globally for its unique properties, we export premium grade crushed leaf, fine powder, and special blends. Processed in sterile indoor facilities with strict quality control to ensure zero contaminants and high alkaloid profiles.
